1.1. Abstract
This document defines how Monopoly-inspired territory and economy mechanics are integrated
into X Paradox X without importing Monopoly as a whole ruleset. It isolates what is adopted,
what is transformed, and what is explicitly excluded.

1.2. Key Words
Monopoly integration, territory ownership, income, upgrade economy, Dominion Points,
hybrid strategy, rule boundaries.

1.3. Executive Summary
X Paradox X integrates Monopoly-like ideas at the systems level: own territory, gain income,
and invest in upgrades. It does not import Monopoly movement, board topology, card decks,
or bankruptcy rules. Integration is constrained to a bounded economic layer that coexists
with chess-piece tactics, dice combat, and 52-card ability effects.

1.4. Key Findings
1.4.1. Territory ownership and recurring income are the primary imported concepts.
1.4.2. Upgrade investment mirrors property development in spirit, not in literal mechanics.
1.4.3. No Monopoly trademarked labels are required to operate the rules system.
1.4.4. Integration is intentionally partial to preserve X Paradox X identity.

1.5. Philosophical Preamble
Integration means selective borrowing, not imitation. The goal is strategic depth from
economic pressure, while preserving the distinct logic of Dark Strategy gameplay.

1.6. Scope and Authority
1.6.1. This file documents conceptual integration boundaries.
1.6.2. Numeric and turn-by-turn mechanic authority remains in 017_game_rules.txt.
1.6.3. If a conflict exists, 017_game_rules.txt is authoritative.

1.7. Imported Monopoly Concepts
1.7.1. Territorial ownership as a persistent state.
1.7.2. Recurring economic benefit from owned territory.
1.7.3. Optional reinvestment into territory upgrades.
1.7.4. Economic advantage as a route toward match victory pressure.

1.8. Adaptation Rules for X Paradox X
1.8.1. "Rent" is adapted into turn-based credits gained during Income phase.
1.8.2. Property set completion mechanics are replaced by board-control and hold-duration logic.
1.8.3. Building upgrades map to Fortress, Market, and Relay as defined in 017_game_rules.txt.
1.8.4. Economic outcomes feed Dominion Points rather than cash-only win conditions.

1.9. Explicit Non-Imports
1.9.1. No roll-and-move traversal around a Monopoly loop board.
1.9.2. No Chance/Community Chest equivalent as a required subsystem.
1.9.3. No mortgage, auction, or bankruptcy mechanics in MVP.
1.9.4. No jail, tax, railroad, or utility subsystem import.

1.10. Integration Points in Game Loop
1.10.1. Income phase: credits awarded per owned territory.
1.10.2. Action phase: optional upgrade purchase on eligible territory.
1.10.3. End-of-turn evaluation: hold-duration contributes to Dominion Points progression.

1.11. Balancing Boundaries
1.11.1. Economy must not dominate tactical play to the point that board positioning becomes
irrelevant.
1.11.2. Tactical combat must remain able to disrupt economic snowball states.
1.11.3. AI adviser recommendations should explain economic opportunity cost explicitly.
