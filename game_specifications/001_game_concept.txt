1.1. Abstract
This document defines the canonical concept, brand identity, and minimum-viable-product
(MVP) scope for X Paradox X, the first title published under the X Dark X company banner in
the Dark Strategy (DS) genre. It supersedes, with explicit reconciliation, the earlier
ideation captured in chatGPT_records/chat_record_30072026.md, which used the working genre
label "eXtended Strategy Game (XSG)" and working title "X Paradox." This document is the
single source of truth for what the game is; all other files in game_specifications/ must
cite this document rather than restate or reinterpret its content.

1.2. Key Words
X Dark X, Dark Strategy, DS, X Paradox X, brand identity, elevator pitch, MVP, hackathon,
LabLab AI Factory, Natively AI, chess, playing cards, dice, territory, economy, tabletop.

1.3. Executive Summary
X Paradox X is a two-player, local pass-and-play digital tabletop strategy game that fuses
standard chess pieces and movement rules, a standard 52-card playing-card deck reinterpreted
as an ability system, six-sided dice combat resolution, and Monopoly-style territory
ownership and economy on a variable-size square board. It is being built for the LabLab AI
Factory hackathon using Natively AI, with this repository (DarkStrategy_XParadoxX, a
py.typed Python package) serving as the authoritative backend implementation target. The
concept intentionally narrows scope to a single vertical slice — one board, two players, a
complete turn cycle, functioning combat, one card resolved, and one victory condition — in
preference to broad but shallow feature coverage, per the delivery priority established in
Section 1.9.

1.4. Key Findings
1.4.1. The brand hierarchy is three-tiered: Company (X Dark X) > Genre (Dark Strategy, DS)
> Title (X Paradox X), and every future title under X Dark X is expected to sit inside the
Dark Strategy genre unless a new genre document is separately ratified.
1.4.2. The game mechanically synthesizes four previously separate systems — positional
chess-like tactics, card-driven abilities, dice-based uncertainty, and territorial economy —
rather than cloning any single existing genre.
1.4.3. This repository is the real Python backend, not a disposable planning artifact; every
subsequent engineering document (005-009, 011-015, 019, 023, 024) governs code that will
actually ship.
1.4.4. Three mechanical pillars were deliberately changed from the original ChatGPT ideation
session during this documentation pass: board size became variable (minimum 4x4) rather than
fixed 6x6; the card system became a standard 52-card deck rather than a bespoke 24-card deck;
and the playable pieces became standard chess pieces with invented combat stat-blocks rather
than four bespoke unit types. See Section 1.8 for the full reconciliation.
1.4.5. The MVP explicitly excludes accounts, online multiplayer, procedurally generated
boards, and real-money economies, matching the original hackathon-scoping guidance in
chatGPT_records/chat_record_30072026.md Section 1 ("Core MVP").

1.5. Philosophical Preamble
A concept document is the deed to a territory not yet built. Before a single line of Python
is written, before Natively AI generates a single component, the founders must be able to
answer, without hesitation: what is this, who is it for, and what will we deliberately not
build first. X Paradox X takes its name and philosophy from the paradox at its center —
that chess's perfect information, cards' hidden information, and dice's raw chance can
coexist in one coherent ruleset without collapsing into noise. Naming the game after that
tension is a statement of intent: rigor and controlled chaos are not opposites here, they are
collaborators.

1.6. Brand Identity

1.6.1. Company
X Dark X is the publishing/production umbrella under which X Paradox X and any future titles
in the Dark Strategy genre are released.

1.6.2. Genre
Dark Strategy (abbreviated DS) is the house genre. It denotes a strategy game blending
positional tactics, resource/territory economy, card-driven ability systems, and bounded
randomness, presented through the X Dark X visual language (see 002_game_aesthetics.txt).
This name and abbreviation supersede the ChatGPT record's working label "eXtended Strategy
Game (XSG)"; XSG is retained only as a historical citation, not as an active brand term.

1.6.3. Title
X Paradox X is the first title in the Dark Strategy genre. This supersedes the ChatGPT
record's working title "X Paradox" (missing the trailing "X").

1.6.4. Tagline
"Own the Board. Break the Rules." — carried forward unchanged from the original ideation
session (chatGPT_records/chat_record_30072026.md).

1.7. Elevator Pitch
X Paradox X is an AI-assisted digital tabletop strategy game combining chess-piece
positional tactics, a standard 52-card ability deck, six-sided-dice combat, and
territory-driven economy on a configurable square board, built as a two-player local
pass-and-play prototype for the LabLab AI Factory hackathon.

1.9. MVP Scope Boundaries

1.9.1. In Scope
1.9.1.1. One configurable square board (default 6x6, selectable 4x4/6x6/8x8/custom).
1.9.1.2. Two local players, pass-and-play, standard chess piece sets scaled to board size.
1.9.1.3. Standard 52-card shared deck with suit/rank-driven abilities.
1.9.1.4. Six-sided-dice combat resolution.
1.9.1.5. Territory capture, income, and Dominion Points economy.
1.9.1.6. One complete, playable turn cycle and at least one working victory condition.

1.9.2. Explicitly Out of Scope (MVP)
1.9.2.1. User accounts, authentication, or profiles.
1.9.2.2. Online/networked multiplayer.
1.9.2.3. Real-money economy or monetization.
1.9.2.4. Procedurally generated (non-deterministic-layout) boards.
1.9.2.5. Fully autonomous AI game-mastering that mutates game state directly (the AI Adviser
described in 016_game_modes.txt only recommends; it never mutates state).
1.9.2.6. Items deferred to 018_future_wishlist_items.txt are, by definition, out of MVP scope.
